This Privacy Notice does not address our privacy practices relating to Delora employees, contractors, or other personnel.
Blockchain participants
Please note that, by virtue of the public nature of blockchains, the holdings, transactions, and other activity associated with a cryptocurrency wallet address may be publicly available and accessible to third parties.
This may include, but is not limited to, public wallet addresses, sender and receiver addresses, token addresses, token amounts, transaction hashes, smart contract interactions, calldata, events, routing or bridge-related information, and any other data included in or associated with a blockchain transaction.
Information stored on a blockchain may not be capable of being modified or deleted due to the immutable nature of blockchain networks. Transactions, addresses, and related blockchain activity may reveal information about a user’s identity and may be correlated, now or in the future, by any party who chooses to do so, including block explorers, RPC providers, analytics providers, indexers, data providers, and other blockchain participants.
Please consider how privacy and transparency on blockchains work before using any blockchain-based service.
